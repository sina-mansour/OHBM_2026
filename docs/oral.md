## Summary

This talk presents the work behind the [companion poster](poster.md): a spectral normative modelling framework applied to **78,405 T1-weighted MRI scans** across the lifespan (ages 5–95), revealing **three principal Thickness Growth Gradients (TGG1–3)** that together explain over 90% of variance in normative cortical thickness trajectories. The gradients dissociate stable global thickness organisation from distinct developmental and aging mechanisms, and align with established cytoarchitectonic, transcriptional, diffusion, and functional cortical hierarchies.

For full author list, affiliations, and the poster itself, see the [poster presentation page](poster.md).
