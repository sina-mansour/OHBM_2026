# OHBM 2026 — Bordeaux

Welcome to my contributions hub for the **OHBM 2026 Annual Meeting** in Bordeaux, France.

This site collects the materials — posters, slides, supplementary content, and project resources — for each of my contributions to the conference.

---

## Contributions

### 🎤 Oral Presentation
**Mapping Organizational Gradients of Cortical Growth Using Spectral Normative Models**

A talk on lifespan cortical growth gradients identified via Spectral Normative Modelling across 78,405 scans.
→ [Read more](oral.md)

### 📌 Poster Presentation
**Mapping Organizational Gradients of Cortical Growth Using Spectral Normative Models**

Companion poster summarising the three Thickness Growth Gradients (TGG1–3) and their alignment with cortical hierarchies.
→ [Read more](poster.md)

### 🎙️ Symposium
**Balancing Sample Size and Sensitivity: Emerging Methods and Tools for Optimal Inference & Power Analysis**

Symposium organised with Martin Lindquist, Stephanie Noble, and Camille Maumet, featuring speakers on methodological advances in neuroimaging power and inference.
→ [Read more](symposium.md)

### 🛠️ Brainhack Project
**SpectraNorm: A Spectral Framework for Normative Modeling of Brain Phenotypes**

An open-source framework for spectral normative modelling, with project goals and contributor onboarding details.
→ [Read more](brainhack.md)
